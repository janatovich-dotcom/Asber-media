Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

AsBer Media ("we", "us", or "the service") provides tools for managing and publishing social media content. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the service or connect a social media account.

2. Information we may process

Depending on how you use the service, we may process account identifiers and authorization information supplied by connected platforms, video files and publishing information that you choose to submit, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

3. TikTok information

If you connect TikTok, AsBer Media processes the information and authorization credentials made available through TikTok's APIs for the purposes you authorize, including authentication and publishing content. We do not sell TikTok account information.

4. Use of information

Information is used to provide the requested publishing functionality, maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers that are necessary to operate the service, where required by law, or where you have authorized the disclosure.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of information held by the service by contacting the service operator.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the service operator using the contact details provided with the service.
