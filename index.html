AsBer Media

A content management and publishing tool for educational and social media content.

What AsBer Media does

AsBer Media helps manage video content and publish it to connected social media accounts, including TikTok, after the account owner authorizes the connection.

The service is intended to simplify the workflow from prepared video content to publication while keeping the account owner in control of authorization and publishing.

TikTok integration

When a user connects a TikTok account, AsBer Media uses TikTok's authorized APIs to authenticate the account and publish eligible video content on the user's behalf. AsBer Media does not represent or belong to TikTok.

Contact

For questions about AsBer Media, privacy, or account data, contact the service operator through the contact information provided with the service.
